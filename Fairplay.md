* Project Name: Fairplay
* Team Members: Jelilat Anofiu (@tjelailah)
* Project Description: Fairplay is a decentralized no-loss prediction market protocol where users can stake on market outcomes without risking their principal. The reward pool is generated from platform fees, which are distributed to winning participants. It also provides a Developer-first SDK that enables developers to integrate no-loss prediction markets in their apps with just a few lines of code.
