Project Name: Nexon

Project Description: Nexon is an AI-driven talent futures market that revolutionizes talent discovery and investment within the Web3 ecosystem. By leveraging AI for scouting and validation, combined with blockchain technology, Nexon enables users to invest in emerging creators through tradable tokens, fostering a decentralized and merit-based approach to talent recognition.

Key Features:





AI-Powered Talent Discovery: Utilizes artificial intelligence to identify and validate emerging talent based on verified achievements rather than social media metrics.



Creator Tokens: Facilitates the creation and trading of tokens representing individual creators, allowing investors to speculate on future prominence.



Scout and Creator Rewards: Incentivizes community participation by offering scouts a 2% fee and creators a 10% fee from trading revenues.



Bonding Curve Implementation: Ensures liquidity and fair pricing for creator tokens through a bonding curve mechanism.

How It Works:





User Onboarding: Users connect their wallets via WalletConnect to access the platform.



Discovery Dashboard: Browse AI-curated profiles of emerging talent.



Scouting: Submit new creator profiles for AI verification and earn rewards upon successful validation.



Investing: Purchase and trade creator tokens, with liquidity managed via quadratic bonding curves.



Profile Management: Creators can claim and manage their profiles, track token performance, and engage with their investor community.
