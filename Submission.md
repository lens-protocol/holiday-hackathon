##Project Description -
MoodMint is a decentralized application that allows users to mint their daily moods as NFTs and share them on the Lens Protocol social graph. Built during the Lens Holiday Hackathon 2024, MoodMint combines emotional expression with blockchain technology to create a unique social experience.
- Connect with Lens Protocol profile
- Create mood-based NFTs with customizable sentiment values
- Generate unique visual representations based on mood
- Share moods directly to Lens Protocol feed
- Track mood history with interactive visualizations
- Create collections of mood NFTs
- View analytics of emotional patterns
